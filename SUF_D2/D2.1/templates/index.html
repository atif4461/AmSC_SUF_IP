Overview

This demonstrator provides a unified operator view for preparing and executing data movement from experimental instruments at Site A to HPC resources at Site B. The workflow includes transfer readiness checks, processing orchestration, and status visibility for key components. Content here is placeholder and can be refined with project-specific architecture, policies, and runbook guidance.
